
# Disaster Relief Coordination and Resource Management System

A centralized platform that helps citizens, volunteers, NGOs, shelter managers, and relief coordinators work together during disasters in Bangladesh.

**Course:** Software Engineering, Summer 25-26 | **Group:** 07, Section A
**University:** American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB)

## Problem

During floods, cyclones, and other disasters, relief information is often scattered across channels and managed manually. This causes delays, duplicate relief distribution, and wasted resources.

## Features

- User registration, login, and role-based access
- Emergency reporting and relief requests with status tracking
- Shelter locator and shelter management
- Volunteer registration and task assignment
- Resource inventory, allocation, and distribution
- Real-time notifications
- NGO and organization management
- Admin dashboard and reports
- Bangla and English support

## User Roles

Citizen, Volunteer, Shelter Manager, Relief Coordinator, NGO / Organization, Administrator, Management

## Process Model

Scrum, with 7 sprints over a 14-week plan (3 weeks pre-game, 10 weeks development, 1 week post-game).
